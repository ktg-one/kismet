PROMPTS AND DIRECTION LOG
=========================

This is a plain-text record of the important prompts, instructions, decisions and direction from the build of the Kismet Finance Group website. It is written so a future AI or developer can pick up the project later without needing the full conversation history.

WHO IS THIS FOR
---------------
The site is for John and Jenny. Normal Australians who earn decent money, own a home, have super and maybe equity. They are not poor and they are not wealthy. They are smart, but they are not finance professionals. They feel financially behind given how hard they work. They want clarity, not sophistication.

Every word, layout choice and image is filtered through John and Jenny.

WHO WROTE THE BRIEF
-------------------
Shane Hewson (Founder/Director) and Josh Hewson (Director) run Kismet Finance Group together. Amy Stoddart joins as EA on 2026-06-01.

Shane is the user. He talks to Claude (this AI) in auto mode for execution. His preferences: short, direct, recommendation plus tradeoff (not options menus), revenue-focused, terse responses.

THE ONE-LINE BRIEF
------------------
"Kismet helps normal Australians understand the bigger picture, connect with the right professionals, and make the process feel clearer."

Three beats. Every page reinforces them.

KEY VOICE EVOLUTION
-------------------

Voice went through several explicit pulls and corrections during the build:

PULL 1: Toward John and Jenny.
Shane flagged drift into "boutique consultancy" cleverness early. Anchor: "would a normal Australian say this at the kitchen table?" Cut bespoke, tailored, curated, harmony, architects, blueprints.

PULL 2: Reference Amplify Solutions Group, but elevate.
Shane sent amplifysolutionsgroup.com.au as competitor reference. Direction: more confident, more modern, less salesy. Use as PROOF that the category can be premium, not as copy to imitate.

PULL 3: Pull back from Amplify imitation.
Once my copy drifted too close ("tailored to your circumstances", "Retain Full Control"), Shane pulled back: hold Kismet's own voice, not Amplify's.

PULL 4: Pull back from writerly drift.
Phrases like "Picture starts moving as one" and "Worked alongside long enough to vouch for" got flagged as too crafted. Replaced with operator voice ("One conversation, one strategy. You stop getting three different versions of the same answer.")

PULL 5: Full language and tone audit.
After multiple pulls, Shane requested a full audit of every page to establish ONE Kismet voice. Result: commit 1429e41 ("copy: full language and tone audit pass"). Killed "Operator Notes", "Strategic Move", "Direct Inquiry", "amidst", "Boutique by design", and similar agency-clever lines.

PULL 6: Kill liability-voice.
Shane flagged: "language like that looks like we are avoiding liability... we want credibility and to position ourselves as great people too deal with but wow these guys know what they are talking about". Result: commit 9809355 ("copy: kill liability-voice, lead with capability"). Killed "we make the introductions and sit in the room", "the conversations stay between you and the specialist", "not us in their seat", "we sit in the middle while they do the regulated work". Replaced with capability-led framing: "we drive the strategy", "we run the play across", "we pull it together", "we keep the conversations connected". Compliance language quarantined to the dedicated Boundaries section on /approach and the ComplianceLine component.

PULL 7: Home page John and Jenny polish.
Shane requested a final polish on home (Hero, BiggerPicture, StrategicPathways, CTA sections) before Vercel. Result: commit f5ee33d. Hero sub now hits the three core beats in one sentence. CTA is "Book a call". "Operator-y" lines like "we work the strategy" and "we run the play across all of them" replaced with plainer phrasing for John and Jenny.

CRITICAL HARD RULES
-------------------

1. NO em-dashes or en-dashes. Anywhere. Ever.
   This is a hard rule from Shane's CLAUDE.md global instructions. Em-dashes and en-dashes are an AI tell that kill credibility. Use periods, commas, parentheses. For ranges write "to" (e.g. "5 to 10").

2. NEVER give regulated financial advice.
   Kismet is an introducer, not a licensed advisor. No "you should..." statements. General observations only. Refer specifics to Dave Caffarelli (licensed broker in the Kismet network).

3. 10:1 value ratio.
   Every prospect-facing message delivers 10x value (insights, framing, stats, story) for every 1 ask. A "touch base" with no value is forbidden.

4. Compliance language quarantined.
   Lives in the dedicated Boundaries section on /approach, the ComplianceLine component, and the footer. Does not bleed into hero or marketing copy.

5. Kismet quality bar = 8.5/10 minimum.
   Premium boutique consultancy standard. Calm, strategic-coordinator framing. Never middleman, never stuffy. Self-check before showing Shane anything.

KNOWN STRUCTURAL ISSUES (workarounds applied)
---------------------------------------------

1. 3CX browser extension hydration error.
   The 3CX extension rewrites tel: and mailto: anchors after SSR, causing React 19 hydration mismatch. Fix: suppressHydrationWarning on every tel: and mailto: anchor in SiteFooter and ContactInquiry.

2. Sharp HEIC decoding fail on Windows.
   Sharp lacks libheif by default on Windows. Fix: heic-convert pure JS package handles the decode step before piping to sharp.

3. Google Reviews scraping blocked.
   Playwright-based Maps page scrape failed against Google's anti-bot protection. Workaround: scripts/scrape-google-reviews.mjs is in the repo as the ingestion entry point, but for now 3 named reviews are curated manually with a "Read all on Google" link. Shane needs to export from Google Business Profile dashboard into public/data/reviews.json for full ingestion.

4. Vercel CLI auth.
   Project is linked but CLI auth requires interactive login. Shane needs to run `vercel login` himself, then the AI can run `vercel --prod`.
