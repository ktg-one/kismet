# Website Direction

## Purpose

The Kismet Finance Group website is the front door of the business. Its job is to convince a warm referral, prospect, partner or recruit, within roughly 30 seconds of landing, that this is a real, premium, capable consultancy worth taking a meeting with. They should leave ready to book a call, not bounce.

The site is not a sales funnel. It is a credibility surface. Conversion happens on the call, not on the page.

## Target audience: John and Jenny

Every word, layout choice and image is filtered through one audience. We call them John and Jenny.

They are normal Australians. They earn decent money. They own a home, have super, maybe some equity. They are not poor and they are not wealthy. They feel financially behind given how hard they work, and they have a quiet sense that the moves are not lining up.

They are not finance professionals. They do not speak in jargon. They feel intimidated by lenders, accountants and advisers. They are smart, but tired of being talked over.

If a sentence on the site cannot be read out loud at a backyard barbecue without sounding like an agency or a bank, it does not belong on the site.

## Positioning

Kismet is a strategic finance coordinator for everyday Australians.

We are not a broker. We are not a financial planner. We are the people who pull the bigger picture together, bring the right professionals into the room, and make the process feel clearer.

Three core beats define the offer:

1. See the bigger picture (lending, property, structure, timing, all in one view).
2. Connect with the right people (broker, accountant, SMSF specialist, project partner).
3. Make the whole process feel clearer (calm pace, plain English, one strategy).

These three beats appear on the home Hero and run through every page underneath.

## Brand tone

Trust, credibility, authority, normal good humans. Premium dark visual, warm conversational copy. Operators who know their stuff and talk like real people. Confident without being arrogant. Authoritative without being intimidating. Boutique by feel, never stuffy.

We sound like the friend who happens to know finance, not the bank manager.

## Design direction

Premium dark theme. Brand navy (#1E3A5F) is the dominant surface colour. Gold (#D4AF37) is the accent, used sparingly for emphasis, hairlines, CTAs and brand marks. Generous space. Editorial rhythm. Photography that feels documentary, not stock.

Detail in the visual layer matches the detail in the copy layer. If the page looks calm and confident, the words must too.

See DESIGN_GUIDE.md for tokens, type, spacing and motion rules.

## What Kismet does

- Coordinates strategy across lending, property, structure and timing.
- Connects clients with vetted partners (brokers, accountants, SMSF specialists, project partners).
- Sits across providers to keep the conversation aligned and the strategy moving.
- Provides general education and framing on how the parts fit together.
- Operates as an authorised representative within the Home Loan Solutions / Australian Finance Group network. Through that network, broker partners can access roughly 70 Australian lenders.

## What Kismet does not do

- Does not provide personal financial advice.
- Does not provide tax advice.
- Does not provide legal advice.
- Does not provide accounting advice.
- Does not hold its own AFSL or Australian Credit Licence.
- Does not sell financial products.
- Does not take fees or commissions from clients.
- Does not pressure clients toward any specific provider.
- Does not promise outcomes, returns or savings.

## Compliance rules (top level, see COMPLIANCE_NOTES.md for detail)

- Regulated work (credit advice, financial product advice, tax structuring) is handled by independently licensed specialists in the Kismet network.
- Australian Finance Group Ltd holds Australian Credit Licence 389087. AFG attribution must be precise wherever the network is referenced.
- All article content carries a general information disclaimer.
- Compliance language is quarantined to the dedicated Boundaries section on the Approach page and the ComplianceLine component. It does not bleed into hero, marketing or trust copy elsewhere on the site.

## What success looks like

A John and Jenny who has never heard of Kismet lands on the home page, scans the Hero in 5 seconds, understands what we do, recognises themselves in the message, and books a call without needing to read anything else.

A partner or referrer sends a prospect to the site and the prospect comes back saying "those guys look the part".

A recruit reads the About page and thinks "I want to work for these people".
